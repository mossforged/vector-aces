VECTORACES

Quick reference

  1. Planning — secretly pick a maneuver for every ship. Red = +1 stress, green = −1 stress.
  2. Activation — lowest pilot skill first: reveal, move, then one action. Stressed ships take no actions and can't fly red.
  3. Combat — highest pilot skill first: attack one enemy inside your arc at Range 1–3. Range 1: +1 attack die. Range 3: +1 defense die. Shooting past an asteroid: +1 defense die.
  4. End — focus & evade tokens are removed (locks and stress stay).

Dice — attack: ✱ hit, ✸ critical, ◉ focus. Defense: ➚ evade, ◉ focus. Each evade cancels a hit (hits before crits). Damage strips shields first, then deals Damage cards (crits face up). A ship dies when its cards reach its hull.

Collisions — moving into a ship stops you touching it and skips your action; touching ships can't shoot each other. Flying over an asteroid skips your action and rolls 1 attack die for damage; you can't attack while sitting on one. Leaving the table destroys the ship.

Ties — the player with initiative (fewer squad points; Dominion on a tie) moves and shoots first. Ships with the same pilot skill as the attacker still fire before being removed.

Board: each range band is 100 mm on a 914 mm (3′) table. Hover attack buttons to see the range line.
